Industry Landscape & Motivation

The Core Problem: Modern Data Tool Fragmentation

1. Extreme Tool Sprawl
  • • Companies juggle 6-8 separate vendors for Ingestion, ETL, BI, Security, and ML.
  • • Engineers spend 80% of their time writing brittle glue code rather than generating insights.
  • • High licensing costs for small and mid-sized enterprises.
2. High Technical Barrier
  • • Non-technical business leaders cannot query databases without SQL/Python expertise.
  • • Dashboard change requests face 2-3 week backlog delays with data teams.
  • • Lack of natural language and voice accessibility.
3. Compliance & AI Lock-In
  • • Sensitive PII (Aadhaar, PAN, Cards) exposed during raw data handling.
  • • Indian DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR mandate immutable tamper-evident audit logs.
  • • Traditional models cannot evolve continually or operate independently.
